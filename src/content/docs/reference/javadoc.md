there is one copy and it cannot fall behind the artifact it documents.

## Artifacts

All modules are published under the `io.github.josipmusa` group on
[Maven Central](https://central.sonatype.com/namespace/io.github.josipmusa). The
`idempotency-bom` artifact manages the versions of all of them - see
[quickstart](/docs/quickstart/).
