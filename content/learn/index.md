These four are about the problem, not the library. They assume you have been handed a
duplicate side effect and now have to decide what to do about it, and they are honest
about the options that are not this library: a processed-events table, a unique
constraint, a distributed lock, a platform.

Read them in order if the whole subject is new. Otherwise take the question that is
yours.
